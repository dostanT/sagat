App blocking

App blocking uses Apple’s Screen Time technology. You choose what to block and you must give permission first. iOS applies the block, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings → Screen Time.

It is an aid, not a lock. Sagat turns the block on while Work is running or paused and off when Work ends. As a safety net, Sagat also arranges for the block to be lifted automatically after a few hours in case the app can’t do it itself — for example, if it was force-quit. We can’t promise that a block will be applied or lifted at an exact moment, because that depends on iOS.

Don’t rely on App blocking for safety, for parental control, or in any situation where being unable to open an app — or being able to — could cause harm. Always keep a way to reach emergency services and the people you need.
